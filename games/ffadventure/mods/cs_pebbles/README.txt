
CSPERSON'S SIMPLE PEBBLES

Hello, thank you for checking out my mod.
It really doesn't do much.
All it does is add a few node types to the game; pebbles, sticks, and other such natural detritus. It doesn't manage generation, or crafting, or anything besides just the nodes themselves.
Pebbles are instantly breakable, have no collision, and cannot "float in midair" (in most cases anyways.)
Originally this just had pebbles. In retrospect, it should've had more; thus it now does. The name still works though.

The idea is that games can generate these nodes in worlds, in biomes where it would make sense for them to exist.
A player could then use those to make a simple axe or something. Therefore avoiding having to "punch trees".
Alternatively, a block of cobblestone could be crafted from pebbles, or pebbles could be crafted from a block of cobblestone. Because cobblestone is... cobbled stones. It just makes sense.
The sticks that end up on the ground naturally are probably pretty flimsy though, realistically speaking. So you'd need something more solid for a tool that will really last. But they'd get you through till you can get something better.

If you want to use this in games, modify it to make it better, or do anything really, feel free. 
It's a very simple mod.



I used the NodeBoxEditor tool to make the models. I also used the "minetest modding book" guide from rubenwardy, and heavily referenced the minetest API documentation.
